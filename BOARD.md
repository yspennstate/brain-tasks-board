WHAT IS PROVED (2026-09-22) - build on it, do not redo it. THE SEPARABLE CASE IS SOLVED with constant exactly pi. If Q is DIAGONAL in the independent coordinates, Q(X) = sum_j lambda_j X_j^2, with >=3 large eigenvalues, then density <= pi, and pi is attained (X1^2+X2^2, uniforms). Proof, elementary and complete: among 3 eigenvalues of modulus >=1, two share a sign (pigeonhole); each square lambda X^2 has density <= y^{-1/2}; two same-sign squares convolve to density <= B(1/2,1/2)=pi (Beta integral); convolving in every other independent variable cannot raise the sup (Young). This is WHY the number is three (it forces a same-sign large pair) and why the constant is pi. SHARPNESS proved: one large eigenvalue gives y^{-1/2} (unbounded); two OPPOSITE-sign give a logarithmic singularity (unbounded).
